# RightPath Math Lab — Project Report

## Topic
AI for Differentiated Learning

## Objective
The objective of RightPath Math Lab is to demonstrate how digital technology can support students with different mathematics skill levels through differentiated practice.

## Implementation
The application focuses on the Pythagorean theorem, a² + b² = c². It provides Foundation, Standard, and Advanced learning levels. Students can read problems, request hints, view worked solutions, and check their answers.

## Differentiation Strategy
The Foundation level uses simple numbers and guided steps. The Standard level requires independent application of the formula. The Advanced level uses a real-world ladder problem that develops mathematical reasoning.

## Technology
The project uses HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and a Vite development setup. The interface is designed to work on mobile devices, tablets, and computers.

## AI Integration and Prompts
The current prototype uses predefined questions and explanations. A live AI model has not yet been connected. The planned AI prompt will ask a language model to generate age-appropriate mathematics problems, provide hints without immediately revealing answers, and adjust explanations according to the student's selected difficulty level.

## Conclusion
RightPath Math Lab demonstrates a practical approach to differentiated mathematics learning. Connecting a real AI model and evaluating its generated responses are the next steps needed to complete the AI integration requirement.
